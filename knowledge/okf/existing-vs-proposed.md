**Before (common clinic friction in the demo narrative):** scattered paper charts, phone-tag for visits, delayed family updates, nutrition advice that lives only in a clinician's notes, and immunization lists that are easy to miss.

**After (Infant Guard demo):** shared digital infant records, clinician-reviewed nutrition suggestions, immunization schedules with reminders, visit booking in-app, and alerts when growth risk flags rise.
