Infant Guard is split into focused modules so each concern stays easy to maintain:

1. User management
2. Newborn registration
3. Medical records and growth tracking
4. Visit scheduling
5. Nutrition plan management
6. Alerts and notifications
7. Growth / nutrition risk analysis

Modules share the database through clear workflows rather than one giant screen.
