The demo defines three roles:

1. **Hospital staff** — registrations, operational monitoring, and report workflows.
2. **Clinicians** — infant charts, visits, nutrition approvals, and medications.
3. **Parents** — read infant summaries, alerts, visit booking, and assigned plans.

Access is role-based. Each role sees only the screens and actions that match their job.
