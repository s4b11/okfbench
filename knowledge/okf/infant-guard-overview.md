**Infant Guard** is a fictional open-source demo product: a web app for **K Brother Children's Hospital** that coordinates newborn care.

It gives clinicians, parents, and hospital staff one place to follow growth, immunization reminders, nutrition plans, and visit schedules.
The demo goal is safer handoffs and fewer missed follow-ups through shared records, early alerts, and clinician-reviewed nutrition suggestions.

In this showcase stack, a Python web backend stores structured records in PostgreSQL (SQLite works locally).
