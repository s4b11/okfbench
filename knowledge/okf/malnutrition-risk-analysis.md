title: Growth and Nutrition Risk Flags
description: Scores concern from growth, nutrition checks, and illness history; alerts
  when a threshold is crossed.
tags:
- module
- risk
---

Looks at growth points, nutrition assessments, and recent illness notes to estimate a concern level.

When the level crosses a configured **threshold**, clinicians and parents get a notice so follow-up can happen sooner. Results also appear on the clinician workspace.
