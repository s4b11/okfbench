Infant Guard's demo goals:

- Keep newborn health information in one shared workspace for K Brother Children's Hospital.
- Register infants and link parent accounts with role-based access.
- Help clinicians review growth trends and history before changing care plans.
- Suggest nutrition plans from simple profile signals, then require clinician approval.
- Notify parents about immunizations, medicines, nutrition updates, and visits.
- Make visit booking and reminders straightforward.
- Flag possible growth or nutrition concerns early for clinician follow-up.
- Improve day-to-day coordination between clinic staff and families.
