Infant Guard includes an assistive drafter that proposes nutrition plans from signals such as **weight, allergy notes, illness history, and growth trends**.

Clinicians **review, edit, approve, or reject** before anything appears for parents. That human review step is required in the demo workflow.

A simple procedure: gather chart signals, match against recommendation templates, store a draft plan, then wait for clinician action.
