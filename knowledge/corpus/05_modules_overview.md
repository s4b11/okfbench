# How the Modules Fit Together

Seven modules keep the demo maintainable: user management, newborn registration, medical records and growth tracking, visit scheduling, nutrition plans, alerts, and growth risk analysis.

User management gates access. Registration creates infant profiles and parent links. Records store measurements and notes that nutrition and risk modules consume. Visits connect families to clinician availability and feed reminder alerts. Nutrition drafts move through clinician review before parents are notified. Risk analysis reads growth and illness signals and can raise alerts on the clinician home view.
