# Assistive Nutrition Drafting

The nutrition module drafts a plan from chart signals, then requires clinician review. Components in the demo include a drafting helper, a review queue, and parent notification after approval.

Typical inputs are weight, allergy notes, illness history, and growth trends. Clinicians may approve, edit, or reject. Only approved plans are shown to parents and mentioned in alerts. This keeps assistive suggestions from becoming unsupervised medical instructions in the demo story.
