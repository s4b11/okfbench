# Goals and People Using the App

Demo goals include a shared newborn workspace, secure family and staff accounts, clinician review of growth history, assistive nutrition drafts that require approval, timely alerts, simple visit booking, and early flags when growth trends look concerning.

People using the app fall into three roles. Hospital staff run registrations and operations. Clinicians manage charts, visits, and plan approvals. Parents view summaries, book visits, and read alerts without needing a phone call for every update.
