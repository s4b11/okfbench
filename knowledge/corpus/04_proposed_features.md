# Feature Walkthrough

Shared infant charts cover birth basics, growth points, medicines, illness notes, and immunizations for authorized users. Alerts appear on dashboards and through an email-style channel for immunizations, medicines, nutrition updates, growth risk, and visits.

Assistive nutrition drafting uses weight, allergy notes, illness history, and growth trends, then pauses for clinician approval. Growth risk scoring watches those signals and notifies people when a threshold is crossed. Immunization schedules run from birth through age five with early reminders. Parents book visits while clinicians manage availability. Staff can publish digital care summaries for download.
