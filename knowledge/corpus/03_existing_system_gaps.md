# Friction the Demo Tries to Reduce

Many clinics still juggle paper charts, ad hoc spreadsheets, and phone-tag for visits. Immunization lists can hide in folders. Nutrition advice may live only in a clinician's memory after a visit. Families hear about due dates late. Growth concerns may be spotted only when someone manually compares measurements.

Infant Guard's story is that shared digital charts, reminders, reviewed nutrition drafts, and threshold-based risk flags reduce that friction. It is a software demo narrative, not a claim about any real hospital's current practice.
